# StremCompat 4.5.1

**The stable version.** It updates by itself from the Homebrew Channel and keeps your data. On LG webOS, choose the same package you use now: `StremCompat-v4.5.1-1080p.ipk`, or `StremCompat-v4.5.1-720p.ipk` for screens under Full HD.

## Fixed

- **Home and Discover show all your catalogs again with a Nuvio profile protected by a PIN.** Typing the PIN at start emptied what the TV keeps of that same profile (its addons, collections and Home order), so Home came up with fewer catalogs until you synced the addons, and then rebuilt itself a few times, jumping back to the start. Entering the profile you already use now keeps everything.
- **Sources load faster again.** 4.5.0 let the TV open only 8 connections at a time to each addon's server, so catalogs, covers and sources waited in line. That limit is gone again.
- **The bolt of a cached source stays visible** on the source in focus.

Everything else in [4.5.0](https://github.com/StremCompat/webos/releases/tag/v4.5.0) stays: torrents that let go when you change sources, a fast Home with collections, watched marks in bulk and the new PIN pad.
