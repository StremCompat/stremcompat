# StremCompat 4.5.0

**The stable version.** It updates by itself from the Homebrew Channel and keeps your data. On LG webOS, choose the same package you use now: `StremCompat-v4.5.0-1080p.ipk`, or `StremCompat-v4.5.0-720p.ipk` for screens under Full HD.

This version fixes the problems reported after 4.4 and some older ones.

## Fixed

- **The app no longer freezes after you change sources** ([#19](https://github.com/StremCompat/webos/issues/19)). A torrent that stalled kept downloading and holding the TV's memory after you left it, so switching between two or three sources could freeze the whole TV until it was unplugged. Now opening another source lets the old one go, leaving the player lets its torrent go after 30 seconds, and Clear torrent cache works as soon as nothing is playing.
- **Reopening the app fixes a stuck start.** A local server that stopped answering kept the app on its loading screen until the TV was unplugged. It is now found and replaced, and the start screen always shows what went wrong.
- **Home with Nuvio collections loads fast.** Collection covers show right away while the TV makes their small copies in the background, so every catalog row appears with them. In our tests with a slow converter, a Home with 36 covers went from about a minute to 2 seconds.
- **Catalog rows no longer disappear** when an addon is slow: a row that fails is asked once more before it is hidden.
- **Try another source** never goes back to a source that already failed in the last few minutes.
- **Marking a whole series or season as watched** is quick, even for long anime. Before, it sent about three requests per episode and could take minutes. **Mark series unwatched** appears again, and episodes not out yet are no longer marked.
- **Episodes from your shows:** a series you hid stays hidden, also as an upcoming episode, and an episode you watched leaves the row at once.
- **Nuvio profiles:** another profile no longer sees the last profile's new episodes, upcoming row or collections. Waiting on "Who is watching?" no longer shows a start error.
- **Nuvio QR sign-in** no longer shows a new code over a sign-in that already worked.
- **Sources:** Recommended now marks the same source the Best button plays, in your preferred language. A torrent without a seeder count is no longer treated as healthy, and the filter counts follow the language and addon filters.
- **Magic Remote:** moving the pointer keeps the screensaver away, and a click only closes it.
- **The Stremio QR code** is replaced by a new one after 10 minutes instead of staying dead.

## New

- **The PIN pad looks like a phone's:** 1 to 9 in rows, with 0 and Delete underneath. It works with the arrows, the number keys and the Magic Remote pointer.

## Android phones (beta)

- The app waits for its server instead of showing the sign-in screen to someone already signed in. The player pauses for calls and when headphones are unplugged.
