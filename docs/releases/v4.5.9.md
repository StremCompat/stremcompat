# StremCompat 4.5.9

**The stable version.** It updates by itself from the Homebrew Channel and keeps your data. On LG webOS, choose the same package you use now: `StremCompat-v4.5.9-1080p.ipk`, or `StremCompat-v4.5.9-720p.ipk` for screens under Full HD.

## New

- **The rating of each episode.** Every episode shows its IMDb rating beside its air date, when the metadata addon has it.
- **Titles open at once.** The title page shows right away what the card already knew (name, picture, synopsis and facts) and fills in the rest when the addon answers, as Stremio and Nuvio do, instead of an empty page.

## Fixed

- **Continue after an update.** The Continue button (S1 E2 and the like) only showed a loading screen and went back after an update or a restart, because the local server forgot the source you were watching. It now keeps the recent sources, and when one is no longer there it opens that episode's sources.
- **Subtitle delay.** The delay is offered again for every subtitle it can move, also those read out of the file.

Everything in [4.5.8](https://github.com/StremCompat/webos/releases/tag/v4.5.8) stays. What changed in each version is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/StremCompat/stremcompat/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
