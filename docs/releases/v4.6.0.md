# StremCompat 4.6.0

**The stable version.** It updates by itself from the Homebrew Channel and keeps your data. On LG webOS, choose the same package you use now: `StremCompat-v4.6.0-1080p.ipk`, or `StremCompat-v4.6.0-720p.ipk` for screens under Full HD.

## New

- **A smoother standard look on slower TVs.** The app measures how your TV keeps up while you use it. Where the transitions make it stutter, it turns off only the motion, as the light look does, and keeps the pictures and the rest of the standard look. TVs that keep up see no change.
- **Your subtitle style is kept.** The size, colour and background you choose in the player stay for the next videos, and Settings shows the same choice.

## Fixed

- **Faster playback.** Since 4.5.9 the app wrote a small file for every piece of a playing video, which slowed older TVs down. It now writes it at most once a minute.
- **Episode ratings with every metadata addon.** With addons like TMDB or AIOMetadata the episodes came without a rating; Cinemeta now completes them. The focused episode's line at the top shows its rating too.

Everything in [4.5.9](https://github.com/StremCompat/webos/releases/tag/v4.5.9) stays. What changed in each version is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/StremCompat/stremcompat/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
