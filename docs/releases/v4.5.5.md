# StremCompat 4.5.5

**The stable version.** It updates by itself from the Homebrew Channel and keeps your data. On LG webOS, choose the same package you use now: `StremCompat-v4.5.5-1080p.ipk`, or `StremCompat-v4.5.5-720p.ipk` for screens under Full HD.

## Fixed

- **The bolt of a cached source looks right on the source in focus.** It repeated over the "TB" letters, like two bolts on top of each other.
- **LG, newer TVs (webOS 10):** the app's service ended itself 5 seconds after starting on TVs that refuse it an activity, and took the local server with it, so the app stopped on "... is not running" ([webos#14](https://github.com/StremCompat/webos/issues/14)). It now keeps running, and the start screen asks the service again twice before showing an error. If it still does not open on your TV, a photo of the start screen helps a lot.

Everything in [4.5.3](https://github.com/StremCompat/webos/releases/tag/v4.5.3) stays (on Samsung, also the fix of [4.5.4](https://github.com/StremCompat/samsung/releases/tag/v4.5.4)). What changed in each version is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/StremCompat/stremcompat/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
