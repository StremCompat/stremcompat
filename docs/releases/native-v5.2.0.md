# StremCompat Native 5.2.0 beta

> **Beta.** The stable version is StremCompat 4.5.0. The Native app installs next to it as a separate app.

Native 5.2.0 brings every fix in 4.5.0 (torrents that let go, a fast Home with collections, watched marks in bulk, the episodes row, Nuvio profiles and sign-in), plus the following.

## New

- **The Magic Remote pointer works on every screen.** The focus follows the pointer within the row you are in, a click presses, holding the click opens the title's menu (like holding OK), and a click outside a menu or panel closes it.
- **The PIN pad looks like a phone's:** 1 to 9 in rows, with 0 and Delete underneath.

## Fixed

- **Trailers and live channels:** the Sources button no longer shows where there are no sources. Pressing it, or OK on a failed trailer, could close the app or open the last title's sources.
- **QR sign-in:** after signing out, the sign-in screen shows a fresh QR code instead of a used one.
- **Player texts:** the audio and subtitles menu says "Subtitles: Off" again (it repeated "Off"), and the next-episode card and the failure screen show their titles.
- **Closing the app during a video** saves where you stopped and lets the torrent go.
- The previous video's HDR setting is no longer used for the next one.
